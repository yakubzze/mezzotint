# Sample images

All images in this folder are **AI-generated**. They were made with Google Gemini
(image generation, square 1:1, 2048 px) on 2026-10-30 for this demo and scaled
down to 1024 px (`thumbs/`: 256 px). They show no real people.

Shared style suffix used for the white-on-black set:

> Pure black background (#000000). Subject rendered only in white and light grey,
> crisp clean silhouette edges, soft rim light that gives some inner tonal detail.
> No text, no logo, no watermark, no border, no frame. Subject centered with
> generous empty black margin on all sides (at least 12% of the frame). Square 1:1.
